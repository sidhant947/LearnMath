Features

100% Offline & Private
- Works entirely without an internet connection.
- Zero analytics trackers, zero telemetry, zero data collection.
- All your progress, streak stats, and mistake drills remain stored securely on your local device.

No Ads, No Microtransactions
- No pop-up ads, banner ads, or paywalled levels.
- Pure, uninterrupted learning and math flow state.

Game Modes
- Level Progression: 60 structured levels spanning Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, and Mixed Boss Fights.
- Daily Challenge: Fresh daily math puzzles to build and maintain your streak.
- Time Attack: 60-second high-speed arithmetic sprint.
- Survival Mode: 3 lives, progressive difficulty, test how far you can go.
- Target 24: The classic 4-number 24 puzzle with unlimited combinations and an integrated solver.
- Local 2-Player Duel: Split-screen head-to-head math battle on a single device.
- Targeted Practice: Custom sessions by category and difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Master).
